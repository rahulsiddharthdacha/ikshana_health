How it works

One call. Complete care.

Shraddha handles the entire wellness check-in automatically — from the call to the counsellor handoff.

01
Employee books or is scheduled
Employees choose their check-in cadence via the portal. EventBridge fires the outbound call at the exact time, no apps needed.
02
Shraddha calls and listens
Amazon Connect dials out. Transcribe Streaming converts speech to text in real time. Claude on Bedrock generates the empathetic reply.
03
Keyword engine scores the call
Every utterance runs through the sensor — green, amber, or red. Red triggers an SNS alert to the on-call counsellor within 30 seconds.
04
SOAP note auto-generated
Post-call, Bedrock reads the full transcript and writes a structured clinical note saved to DynamoDB for the care team.
05
HR sees anonymised trends
QuickSight surfaces population wellness scores, risk distribution, and department heatmaps — no individual data exposed to HR.
06
Everything stays in India
100% AWS ap-south-1. All audio, transcripts, and notes encrypted with KMS. DPDP-aligned. BAA available from AWS.
